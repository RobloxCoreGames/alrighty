# Wither Storm

A Three.js take on the Wither Storm from Minecraft: Story Mode. Open `index.html` in a browser (three.js loads from cdnjs, so no build step is needed).

- A blocky three-headed body around a glowing Command Block
- Tentacles made of tumbling blocks, plus debris orbiting the storm
- A purple tractor beam that rips blocks out of the ground and pulls them into the center head
- Three growth stages, a Roar button, lightning and storm clouds
- Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom
